# Scope research: what the first research missed (2026-10-31)

**The question.** Dan asked what the first research missed: "a dynamic framework for any kind of work that involved AI to have well researched bounderies for each for enterprise and leadership, architecture of any … kind" ([brief](../brief/2026-10-31.md)). He added energy and running costs per element ([brief](../brief/2026-10-31-2.md)) and a frame: Hyleon "connects things after research has been done … connect the dots on what ai does or gets" ([brief](../brief/2026-10-31-3.md)).

**How.** Six research agents, one category each. Their reports, never edited, are the appendices:

1. [Requirements management and MBSE](2026-10-31-scope/1-mbse-requirements.md)
2. [Network design and verification](2026-10-31-scope/2-network-verification.md)
3. [Model checking and code compliance](2026-10-31-scope/3-model-code-checking.md)
4. [Enterprise architecture and AI governance](2026-10-31-scope/4-ea-and-ai-governance.md)
5. [Software guardrails, compliance automation and leadership tools](2026-10-31-scope/5-software-compliance-leadership.md)
6. [Energy, operating cost, lifecycle cost and carbon](2026-10-31-scope/6-energy-cost-lifecycle.md)
7. [Civil, plant and vector drawing tools](2026-10-31-scope/7-civil-plant-and-vector-tools.md), added at Dan's request for the tools the first six didn't cover

**Limits.** Every agent ran out of search budget partway. Claims seen only in summaries are marked unverified in the appendices.

## The finding

**In every category, the checking engines are built, well funded, and now sold as a "connecting layer" for AI agents, with MCP servers.** Examples:
- Flow Engineering ($50M, 2026-09)
- Forward Predict (GA 2026-09-29)
- Ardoq
- Vanta ($4.15B valuation)
- ABACUS (whose CEO, on 2026-10-21, called the architecture map "the rules and briefing agents work from")

**What none of them ships is the research itself:** open, sourced, tested boundaries for a domain, checked against what an AI actually produced, with every value marked as guessed until a person confirms it. Each customer writes their own rules, vendors write closed libraries, or the rules cover the AI system rather than its work.

**No one covers Hyleon's original ground:**
- panels, controls, OT networks and piping, checked against NEC or UL 508A, at the physical layer
- energy, cost and carbon for equipment, from a design, with a source on every value

## What it means for the scope

These are options, not a decision; the decision and any TRUTHS.md wording are Dan's.

| Option | What it is | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Physical first, as written** | TRUTHS unchanged. Add energy and cost, and plug into specialists (Batfish, SysML v2, IDS) | Uncontested ground; the research confirms the gaps | Leaves Dan's wider idea unstated |
| **B. General framework now** | "Researched boundaries for any AI-assisted work", with enterprise and leadership packs early | Matches the wider idea | Crowded, consolidating markets with large buyers; every domain needs its own researched content; buyers there want blocking |
| **C. Open pack standard, physical packs first** | The product connects researched packs to the work and to what AI produces. The pack format is open, so other domains come through other authors or later packs. Hyleon ships the physical packs first | Matches "connect the dots" and the engine that's already designed; avoids fighting the giants; the unclaimed piece (open, sourced, tested packs) becomes the moat | A truth change (Dan's wording); an open format needs governance; reach depends on pack authors |

## Decisions only Dan can make
- **Scope:** A, B or C, and the TRUTHS.md wording if it changes.
- **The buyer:** site trades and integrators, owners, enterprises, or a mix.
- **The pack format:** open as a standard others can write in, or Hyleon's alone.
- **Who researches** packs outside physical design.
- **Enterprise buyers who want blocking:** does "inform, never block" hold?
- **Energy and cost:** whether they belong in Hyleon, and at which phase. Appendix 6 suggests a minimal phase 4.
- **Code text:** the user's own licence or agent, licensed from ICC or NFPA, or citations only.
- **The name "packs":** Credo AI uses "policy packs".

## Addendum: appendix 7

Civil 3D, Bentley (OpenRoads, OpenFlows, OpenPlant) and Hexagon/Octave check designs only inside their own tools, against rules the customer or an agency sets:
- they mostly warn
- none records a source for each limit or asks before designing
- none covers control panels or OT networks
- they overlap Hyleon only at the edges: buried pipe and hydraulics at a pump station, and plant piping for large firms

Bentley's Substation+ (early access, 2026-05-20) comes closest to a connecting layer: its AI checks a model against criteria the engineer uploads. It covers substations only, and it's closed.

The exchange formats are LandXML, IFC 4.3, DEXPI 2.0.1 (released 2026-09-30), SWMM and SVG. Inkscape and draw.io check nothing; since 2026-09, agents can draw in Inkscape through community MCP servers.

A new decision for Dan: what "site systems" in D-001 covers, because civil tools use "site" for grading and drainage.
